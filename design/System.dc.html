Principles

Why it looks like this
Show the mechanism

Chunks, similarity scores and cluster counts are visible, not hidden behind a spinner. Trust in retrieval comes from seeing what was retrieved.

Monospace means measured

If a number came from the machine — a score, a count, a timestamp — it is set in mono. Prose stays in the sans.

Rules, not shadows

Structure comes from hairlines and ground changes. Elevation is reserved for things genuinely floating above the page.

Admit the gaps

The library says what it does not contain. An honest “nothing saved on this” beats a confident paragraph.
